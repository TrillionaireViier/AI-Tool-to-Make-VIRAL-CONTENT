Good Questions.

A few things you might be wondering about Poppy.

What exactly is Poppy?

Poppy is an AI-powered content workspace that helps creators, founders, and marketers organize their ideas, explore new angles, and turn their knowledge into engaging content.

How is Poppy different from a generic AI chatbot?

Instead of starting from a blank chat, Poppy is designed around your projects and source material. It helps you bring context together and develop ideas into content that reflects your voice and expertise.

What can I upload into Poppy?

You can bring in your working materials—such as notes, documents, research, and links—so your creative process starts with the ideas and information that matter to you.

What kind of content can Poppy create?

Use Poppy to brainstorm and develop social posts, newsletters, scripts, articles, and other written content. You stay in control of the final wording and where it gets published.

Does Poppy work for agencies?

Yes. Poppy can support agency teams working across multiple clients and projects by helping keep source material, ideas, and content workflows organized. Your team can shape each deliverable to fit its client.
